# Notice Ledger

## Check the barriers before writing the bid

An independent research service for Canadian research and analytics suppliers. Each brief checks public tender notices and selected attached solicitation documents for closing dates, qualification restrictions and information that still needs confirmation.

**[Read the sample screening brief](SAMPLE-BRIEF.md).** It covers three real notices checked on October 18, 2026. One requires an existing supply arrangement; another requires two people and evidence of prior standards work. Those details matter more than a matching keyword.

## Proposed offer: C$79 for one screening package

One package covers up to three customer-selected Canadian federal notices and up to 60 pages of publicly accessible attached documents in total. The exact documents and page ranges are agreed before any order. It includes:

- A dated screening table with source links and page references.
- Documented qualification barriers, deadlines and scope mismatches.
- Questions the supplier needs to resolve before deciding to bid.

The document list states which attachments and page ranges were checked, which were unavailable, and what remains unreviewed. This is a selected entry-barrier check, not a complete compliance audit.

This is document screening, not professional procurement advice. Legal advice, bid writing and submission are not included. It does not certify eligibility, cover every amendment, or predict a contract award. The buyer remains responsible for consulting the current official documents.

**Early-access validation only. Checkout is not active, no payment is requested, and no delivery booking is being accepted.** The C$79 price is a proposed price being tested, before any applicable tax.

To express interest, [open a request](https://github.com/honmrie/notice-ledger/issues/new?template=interest.md). Share public notice URLs and the type of work you supply. Do not post private business documents, credentials, personal identifiers or confidential bid material. An expression of interest creates no obligation to buy.

## Evidence behind the sample

The public CanadaBuys export used for this sample contains 899 records marked Open. A calendar-date check against October 18, 2026 found 843 with a later closing date, 55 with an earlier closing date and one closing that day. These are snapshot findings, not a claim that the underlying notices are wrong or that all 843 notices are available to a particular supplier.

The [audit record](data/2026-10-18/audit.json) contains the source URL, data hash and method. The [date-screen export](data/2026-10-18/date-screen.csv) includes no officer contact details or tender attachments.

## Refresh and checks

The routine data check uses Python's standard library and public data. The screening brief is a separate document and is not generated by the date checker.

```sh
python3 -m unittest -v test_refresh.py
python3 refresh.py --as-of YYYY-MM-DD --output data/YYYY-MM-DD
```

Choose the actual review date and a new output directory for each snapshot. The script stops on a changed schema, duplicate reference, empty dataset or malformed CSV. It keeps same-day closing dates separate because the export's timestamps do not carry time zones. Original notice pages must be consulted for exact closing times.

No background refresh or email subscription is active. This sample becomes stale as notices and documents change.
